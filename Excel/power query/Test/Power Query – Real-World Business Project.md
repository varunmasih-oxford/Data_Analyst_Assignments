# 2. Business Problems – 20 Questions

## Module 1: Data Cleaning and Preparation

### Q1. Consolidate Monthly Sales

The company receives sales records from different branches.

Prepare one consolidated sales table containing January and February transactions.

### Q2. Remove Duplicate Transactions

Some transactions have been entered multiple times.

Identify duplicate transactions and prepare a dataset containing unique orders.

### Q3. Standardize Customer Names

Customer names contain inconsistent capitalization, extra spaces and unwanted characters.

Prepare a standardized customer list.

### Q4. Handle Missing and Incorrect Values

Some sales records contain:

- Blank CustomerIDs
- Missing quantities
- Missing prices
- Incorrect values

Prepare a reliable sales dataset for reporting.

### Q5. Standardize Data Formats

The company receives dates, quantities and prices in different formats.

Standardize all columns so they can be used for calculations and reporting.

---

## Module 2: Data Transformation

### Q6. Extract Customer Information

A new customer report contains information in the format:

`C101-Delhi-Retail`

Management wants CustomerID, City and Segment in separate columns.

### Q7. Calculate Total Sales

Management wants to calculate the total sales amount for every transaction and identify the highest-value orders.

### Q8. Calculate Transaction Profit

The company wants to calculate the profit generated by each transaction using product cost and selling price.

### Q9. Prepare Date-Based Analysis

Management wants to analyse sales by:

- Year
- Month
- Quarter
- Day

Prepare the sales data accordingly.

### Q10. Categorize Sales Transactions

Management wants to classify orders into Low, Medium and High sales categories based on their transaction value.

---

## Module 3: Business Reporting

### Q11. Identify Valid Sales Transactions

Management wants a report containing only valid transactions with positive quantities and sales above ₹500.

### Q12. Prepare Product Performance Report

Prepare a product performance report showing:

- Total quantity sold
- Total revenue
- Average selling price
- Total profit

### Q13. Prepare Region-Wise Product Sales

Management wants to compare product sales across different regions.

Prepare a report with regions as rows and individual products as columns.

### Q14. Restructure Monthly Sales Report

The company receives monthly reports in which January, February and March sales are stored in separate columns.

Prepare a report where each month appears as a separate record.

### Q15. Combine Monthly Sales Files

The company has started receiving monthly sales files from a new branch.

Prepare a system that combines all monthly files into one sales dataset.

---

## Module 4: Data Integration and Analysis

### Q16. Prepare Customer Purchase Analysis

Management wants to identify which customers are purchasing the most.

Prepare a sales report containing:

- Customer names
- Cities
- Customer segments
- Total purchases

### Q17. Prepare Complete Sales Information

Management wants a complete sales report containing:

- Product details
- Product categories
- Branch names
- Branch managers

### Q18. Analyse Product Returns

Management wants to understand the financial impact of product returns.

Tasks:

- Calculate the value of returned products.
- Calculate net sales after returns.
- Identify the most frequently returned products.
- Prepare a return reason summary.

Formula:

Net Sales = Gross Sales - Returned Amount

---

## Module 5: Advanced Business Requirements

### Q19. Create Dynamic Region-Wise Analysis

The management wants to select a region and view its sales performance without manually modifying the source data each time.

Prepare a solution that allows management to change the selected region and refresh the results.

### Q20. Build an Automated Reporting System

The management wants a complete automated reporting system.

Requirements:

- New monthly sales files should be added to a company folder.
- The consolidated dataset should update after refreshing.
- Customer, product and branch information should be included.
- Gross sales, profit and net sales should be calculated.
- Business summaries should be updated automatically.
- The final output should be ready for reporting.
